The Divine Abode

Braj Bhoomi — The Playground of Sri Radha Krishna

Mathura and Vrindavan are the sacred lands where Lord Krishna performed His mystical childhood pastimes (leelas) and eternal love-plays with Sri Radha Rani. Living in this holy region keeps you close to peaceful vibrations and centuries-old heritage.

Banke Bihari Temple The spiritual heartbeat of Vrindavan housing the beloved deity of Thakur Ji. (10 mins away)
Prem Mandir The magnificent Temple of Divine Love depicting Krishna's childhood leelas. (12 mins away)
Krishna Janmabhoomi The historical and sacred birthplace of Lord Krishna in Mathura. (15 mins away)
Govardhan Hill The holy hill lifted by Lord Krishna, famous for the 21km parikrama walk. (25 mins away)
Nidhivan & Seva Kunj Sacred forest groves where the divine couple is believed to perform Rasleela. (11 mins away)
Yamuna River Ghats Tranquil banks for sacred bathing and viewing the divine evening Aarti. (15 mins away)
